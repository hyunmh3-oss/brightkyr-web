<div class="contain">
				<div class="topBox">
					<p>Leading University Project for International Cooperation (a.k.a. LUPIC) aims to implement an advanced nursing education program <br>
					in Kyrgyz Republic to produce highly skilled nurses and enhance the community's ability to effectively respond to infectious diseases, <br>leading to improved healthcare and population health in Kyrgyz Republic.<br>
					Innovation in nursing degree program on pandemic disaster response and clinical competence <br>(2022. 04 – 2029. 03, National Research Foundation, Ministry of Education)</p>
