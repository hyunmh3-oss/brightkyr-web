<div class="contain">
				<div class="topBox">
					<p>The child health promotion project aimed to support a child health promotion and also to ensure a healthy and happy life for <br>
					all in Kyrgyz Republic. We have worked to enhance the level of child health promotion services in migrant village areas through <br>
					resident participation and the development of the skills of healthcare professionals.<br>
					Bright Kyrgyzstan: Community-based health promotion project for children from immigrant villages, Kyrgyzstan <br>(2017 – 2019, Korea International Cooperation Agency, Ministry of Foreign Affairs)</p>
				</div>
			</div>

						<h4 class="tit">Goals</h4>
					</div>
					<div class="contents">
						<div class="objective-list">
							<div class="row">
								<div class="img"><img src="../images/sub/obj1_1.jpg" alt=""></div>
								<div class="txt bg1">
									<div class="cell">
										<div class="tit">Development of child health promotion service system</div>
										<div class="desc">
											<ul class="dot-list">
												<li>• Developed a child health promotion service manual, including child health screening tools, and have built an MCS to effectively use it</li>
												<li>• Organized Parent Leader group to promote children's health by connecting parents and nurses of the healthcare center</li>
												<li>• Established a child health promotion center to hold activities<br> for chiild health promotion, where medical staff and residents<br> are conducting various activities</li>
											</ul>
										</div>
									</div>
								</div>
							</div>
							<div class="row">
								<div class="img"><img src="../images/sub/obj1_2.jpg" alt=""></div>
								<div class="txt bg2">
									<div class="cell">
										<div class="tit">Strengthening the potential of healthcare professionals</div>
										<div class="desc">
											<ul class="dot-list">
												<li>• Training was conducted on the topic of child health, parenting environment, and medical service for community health workers </li>
												<li>• Through academic exchanges with the Nursing department in Bishkek, participation in child health promotion services, and connection with practical educational institutions,<br>  we have created a virtuous cycle of strengthening the capacity<br> of the health and medical personnel</li>
											</ul>
										</div>
									</div>
								</div>
							</div>
							<div class="row">
								<div class="img"><img src="../images/sub/obj1_3.jpg" alt=""></div>
								<div class="txt bg3">
									<div class="cell">
										<div class="tit">Strengthening community participation capabilities</div>
										<div class="desc">
											<ul class="dot-list">
												<li>• In order to inspire residents ' understanding and awareness of child health issues, we have activated public and private resources to help them engage in and have the capacity to digest ongoing child health promotion activities themselves</li>
												<li>• We have established a community-based operating organization and expanded the scope of beneficiaries through awareness improvement activities for child health and increased the participation<br> of residents in voluntary health promotion activities</li>
											</ul>
