<p class="MsoNormal"><span lang="EN-US">As previously noted, the introduction of
Bachelor's and graduate degree programs in nursing and midwifery was finalized
at a cabinet meeting held by the Republic of Kyrgyzstan on May 29.<o:p></o:p></span></p><p class="MsoNormal"><span lang="EN-US">This achievement which was achieved in
collaboration between BK-LUPIC and I. K. Akhunbaev KSMA has been reported in
various media outlets in South Korea.<o:p></o:p></span></p><p class="MsoNormal"><span lang="EN-US">The national approval of the introduction
of Bachelor's and graduate degree programs in nursing and midwifery is expected
to contribute to the improvement of standards of national healthcare system of
the Kyrgyz Republic by developing and operating quality Bachelor's programs in
nursing education in the future! <o:p></o:p></span></p><p>
