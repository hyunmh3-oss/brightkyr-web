the organizations organizing the event. The forum will be held on <b>May 7th from
09:30 a.m. (KGT) at the Conference Hall of I.K. Akunbaev Kyrgyz State Medical
Academy, Bishkek, Kyrgyz Republic</b>.<o:p></o:p></span></p><p class="MsoNormal"><span lang="EN-US">&nbsp;</span></p><p class="MsoNormal"><span lang="EN-US">&nbsp;As a result of ongoing reforms in the field

responsibilities, and positions at all levels of healthcare organizations. Florence
Nightingale once said, "The most important thing in hospitals is not to
harm the patients." In hospitals, nurses collaborate with various
healthcare professionals to promote patient recovery. Interprofessional
collaboration among healthcare teams enables accurate diagnosis and treatment,
ensuring safety and comfort for patients, and fosters medical innovation and
research.<o:p></o:p></span></p><p class="MsoNormal"><span lang="EN-US">This forum will provide participants with
