International Cooperation (Bright Kyrgyzstan – LUPIC; Director Professor
Hyunsook Shin) project group received a certificate of appreciation from the
Kyrgyz State Medical Academy (KSMA) in celebration of KSMA’s 85th anniversary.</span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal" style="margin-bottom: 0.0001pt; line-height: normal; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; word-break: keep-all;"><span lang="EN-US" style="mso-bidi-font-size:11.5pt;

&quot;Segoe UI&quot;;color:black;mso-font-kerning:0pt">KSMA, a leading institution in
healthcare education in the Kyrgyz Republic, has been at the forefront of
producing healthcare professionals with exceptional practical and research
capabilities. The relationship between Bright Kyrgyzstan and KSMA began in 2017
through a community-based child health promotion project in migrant areas of
the Kyrgyz Republic. KSMA participated as an advisory committee member in this
project, collaborating to establish a child health promotion service system and
enhance the capabilities of healthcare personnel and community participation. From
2019 to 2021, the two institutions worked together on a global education
support project to improve nursing education. This involved developing a master
plan, enhancing practical training, and formulating policy improvements for
nursing education to ensure the training of competent healthcare professionals.
Since 2022, they have been collaborating on the BK-LUPIC project to establish
an advanced nursing education model and strengthen community infection response
capabilities in the Kyrgyz Republic. This project is set to continue until
2029.<o:p></o:p></span></p>
