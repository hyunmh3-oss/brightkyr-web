<p>On July 16, 2020, we held an online workshop titled "Analysis of the practical course of nursing education in KSMA", under the project "Improvement the practice of nursing practicum education in KSMA". During the workshop, we were able to listen to Professor Suk Jeong Lee's (Chung-Ang University, Republic of Korea) lecture on the nursing practical course where in information on conducting practical courses in Korea was provided in details. In the next session, we considered an analytical review of the practical course of the nursing department of KSMA.</p><p>We would like to thank Professor Lee for the informative lecture, as well as the KSMA nursing department teachers for their diligence and detailed analysis. Also, we would like to express our sincere gratitude to the representatives of the MoH of the Kyrgyz Republic, WHO, the project "Reforms of Medical Education in the Kyrgyz Republic" and the professors of nursing department in Korea, who also participated from different universities.&nbsp;</p><p>Thank you very much for your interest, support and participation, despite your busy schedule during this pandemic.
