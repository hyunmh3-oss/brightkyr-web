<p class="MsoNormal"><span lang="EN-US">The BK-LUPIC team met the key stakeholders
of the WHO Kyrgyzstan regional office.<o:p></o:p></span></p><p>

</p><p class="MsoNormal"><span lang="EN-US">A meeting was held to share the status and
achievements of the BK-LUPIC team and the WHO Kyrgyzstan office, and to discuss
future cooperation. BK-LUPIC shares a vision in the same context as the WHO's
2030 Nursing Education Roadmap. BK-LUPIC intends to continue cooperation needed
for a healthier Kyrgyz Republic through the establishment of a WHO
collaborative center for cooperation with BK-LUPIC, KSMA, and the improvement
of health information systems.<o:p></o:p></span></p><p class="MsoNormal"><span lang="EN-US"><br></span></p><p class="MsoNormal"><img src="/images/uploaded/summernote/202308/488461bfde88fdf4a8ce4254ddc78ae4.jpg" style="width: 800px;"><span lang="EN-US"><br></span></p><p class="MsoNormal"><img src="/images/uploaded/summernote/202308/aaf6992022ec8024dbdb11854fadd80f.jpg" style="width: 800px;"><br></p>
