<p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:40.0pt"><span lang="EN-US">The
4th International Nursing Forum, held on June 27th, 2023, at the Kyrgyz State
Medical Academy, focused on the theme of "Nursing Leadership and Career
Development." The forum aimed to emphasize the importance of nursing
education for career advancement and explore global strategic directions for
nurturing nurse leaders. It also aimed to create a platform for exchange and
interaction among nurses, nursing professors, policymakers, and representatives
from civil society and international organizations, encouraging participation
from various healthcare practice and education fields to enrich the depth and
breadth of discussions and interactions at the forum. The forum included
lectures, panel discussions, and a student forum.<o:p></o:p></span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:40.0pt"><span lang="EN-US">The
event was graced by the presence of Ambassador Lee Won-jae from the Embassy of
the Republic of Korea in the Kyrgyz Republic, Mamadaliev Tilek Abdyganievich,
Deputy Minister of Health of the Kyrgyz Republic, Usekov Ulanbek
Abdilazizovich, Deputy Minister of Education and Science of the Kyrgyz
Republic, who delivered congratulatory remarks. Representatives from major
government agencies and ministries including the Ministry of Health and the
Ministry of Education and Science of the Kyrgyz Republic, as well as healthcare
institutions, educational institutions, relevant associations, and
international organizations, graced the event with their presence. Over 550
stakeholders participated, with more than 450 attendees present in person and
over 100 participants joining online. Their valuable presence added luster to the
forum and contributed to its success.<o:p></o:p></span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent:40.0pt"><span lang="EN-US">The
forum aimed to provide a comprehensive perspective on nursing in the Kyrgyz
Republic from various fields, not limited to the nursing domain alone. There
were diverse thoughts and viewpoints on nursing from different stakeholders,
but a shared understanding emerged regarding the importance of nursing
competence development and growth in elevating the country's healthcare
standards. The need for education and training in this regard was emphasized.
The active voices of nurses working in the field were also heard, contributing
valuable insights. Based on the discussions at the forum, meaningful
resolutions were derived, taking into account the opinions and consensus of the
participants.<o:p></o:p></span></p><p>

would like to express our gratitude to all the participants who took their
valuable time to attend the 4th International Nursing Forum. We hope that it
was a meaningful and enriching experience for everyone involved. We also extend
our thanks to all the individuals who contributed to the organization and
smooth operation of the forum.
