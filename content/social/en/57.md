<p class="MsoNormal"><span lang="EN-US">We are delighted to bring you some
remarkable news that will brighten the future of nursing education in the
Kyrgyz Republic! During the most recent cabinet, it has been decided to
introduce bachelor's and graduate degree programs in nursing and midwifery.
This momentous decision marks a significant milestone for our national
healthcare system.<o:p></o:p></span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal"><span lang="EN-US">This incredible achievement is a testament
to the unwavering dedication and tireless efforts of BK-LUPIC and I. K.
Akhunbaev KSMA in close collaboration with the Ministry of Education and
Science and the Ministry of Health. Through their collective commitment, the
Kyrgyz Republic will now witness the establishment of its first-ever bachelor's
and graduate degrees in nursing. <o:p></o:p></span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal"><span lang="EN-US">The introduction of these new degree
programs signifies a profound opportunity for nurses in the Kyrgyz Republic to
pursue higher education, expanding their knowledge and skills. As a result, we
anticipate a significant elevation in their competence and professionalism,
while concurrently raising public awareness about the invaluable role nurses
play in our society. Empowering our nurses in this manner will undoubtedly
contribute to substantial improvements in the quality and standards of our
national healthcare system.<o:p></o:p></span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal"><span lang="EN-US">Over the past year, BK-LUPIC and I. K.
Akhunbaev KSMA have meticulously developed and obtained approval for the first
Bachelor's degree program in nursing. KSMA is now actively preparing to
commence this program in September!<o:p></o:p></span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal"><span lang="EN-US">We kindly request the continued support and
attention of our advocates for BK-LUPIC's endeavors to nurture competent nurses
in the Kyrgyz Republic. Together, we possess the power to shape a brighter
future for our healthcare system and ensure the well-being of all our
