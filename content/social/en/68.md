<p><span style="font-size: 14.4px;">To define the areas and direction of cooperation in order to systematize health information systems for strengthening community infection response capacity, we had a meeting with E-Health Center Director Chumachok Vitalii. and Vice-Director Beksultanov Chingiz. In order to ensure the smooth deployment of the health information system in the Kyrgyz Republic, we agreed to move together for the integration of the medical information system based on the training and education of the Outpatient Record Card System and the development and introduction of the Inpatient Record Card System. In order for the health information system in the Kyrgyz Republic to be used efficiently and respond effectively to health and medical crisis situations, the BK-LUPIC team and KSMA will keep cooperating with e-health center.</span><br></p><p><span style="font-size: 14.4px;"><br></span></p><p><img src="/images/uploaded/summernote/202308/43b14b628cb53f08fcbb1e9a099efb8c.jpg" style="width: 800px;"><span style="font-size: 14.4px;"><br></span></p><p><img src="/images/uploaded/summernote/202308/d1dbb033701ff0a51010ac04b5dc1e8a.jpg" style="width: 800px;"><br></p>
