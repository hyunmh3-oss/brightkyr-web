<p class="MsoNormal">Deputy Minister of Education and Science of the Kyrgyz
Republic Bekzahan Supanaliev, Higher, Secondary, and Postgraduate Education
Department Expert Asan Kyzy Ainura, International Cooperation Department
Apysheva Aizada Ishenbekova, Sultanov Ilgiz, and other key stakeholders
gathered with the Korean and Kyrgyz team <span lang="EN-US">of </span>BK-LUPIC and members of the GKNF International Development Cooperation
Executive Committee.<o:p></o:p></p><p class="MsoNormal">They celebrated the successful introduction of
bachelor's and master's programs in nursing through cooperation between the
Ministry of Education and Science and BK-LUPIC. They also discussed the
importance of BK-LUPIC's plans for operating undergraduate programs and
introducing master's programs.<o:p></o:p></p><p>



</p><p class="MsoNormal">The Ministry of Education and Science affirmed that
they will continue to support and cooperate with the BK-LUPIC team in their
journey to improve nursing and healthcare education in the Kyrgyz Republic,
solidifying a strong partnership.<o:p></o:p></p><p class="MsoNormal"><img src="/images/uploaded/summernote/202308/523344a55998d65c7db384c744a690a9.jpg" style="width: 695.139px;"><br></p><p class="MsoNormal"><img src="/images/uploaded/summernote/202308/6bd83a7baeb43b55fd0787a343af6e71.jpg" style="width: 714.028px;"><br></p><p class="MsoNormal"><br></p>
