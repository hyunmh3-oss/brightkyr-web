<p>Hello. it's been a long time to update 'Bright Kyrgyzstan - Global Education Support' project feeds so we are the bearer of good tidings today.</p><p><br></p><p>On Aug 25th the'Bright Kyrgyzstan - Global Education Support' (a.k.a. BK-GES) project team participated in the "Congress of Nurses &amp; Midwives of Kyrgyz Republic" which was held in the Issyk-Kul region. Professor Hyunsook Shin joined as a speaker of the first plenary session and shared an implementation of project also achievements of the BK GES team over the past three years under theme of "Higher Nursing Education Reforms in Kyrgyz Republic. " Is there anyone watched and joined the congress? More than 500 people from a diverse area including nurses, professors in nursing education institutions, officials of government, nurse associations, and international NGOs gathered together at Congress via online and offline. It was a meaningful time for us to experience that Kyrgyz's nursing and nursing education could be improved, and that our quality of nursing would be promoted soon.</p><p>'Bright Kyrgyzstan - Global Education Support' project news will be updated soon or late and&nbsp; hope to be you stay safe and healthy.&nbsp;</p><p><img src="/images/uploaded/summernote/202302/52036c355085f77778b595edc8222ee5.jpg" style="width: 589px;"><img src="/images/uploaded/summernote/202302/7027353151e59ed037579ad35a150221.jpg" style="width: 606px;"><img src="/images/uploaded/summernote/202302/47f5d22d2b5b507de31a6704c00cb4a2.jpg" style="width: 623px;"><img src="/images/uploaded/summernote/202302/4fbf54881a94fec34c58369e78d9d969.jpg" style="width: 640px;"><img src="/images/uploaded/summernote/202302/1c09c0f9512ef8e8d6e347f7a9929a15.jpg" style="width: 657px;"><br></p>
