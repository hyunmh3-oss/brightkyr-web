<p class="MsoNormal" align="left" style="margin-bottom: 0cm; line-height: normal; word-break: keep-all;"><span lang="EN-US">For seven days from July 18th to July
24th, the "July 2023 Faculty Development Workshop" was held in Abu
Dhabi, focusing on enhancing the research capacity of the faculty members of I.
K Akhunbaev Kyrgyz State Medical Academy (KSMA).<o:p></o:p></span></p><p class="MsoNormal" align="left" style="margin-bottom: 0cm; line-height: normal; word-break: keep-all;"><span lang="EN-US">&nbsp;</span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="mso-pagination:widow-orphan;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric ideograph-other;

University and KSMA attended the Sigma Theta Tau International 34th
International Nursing Research Congress and presented the results of 3 collaborative
research projects conducted over the past year. <o:p></o:p></span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="mso-pagination:widow-orphan;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric ideograph-other;
word-break:keep-all"><span lang="EN-US">Through this, we shared the current
status and direction of improving nursing education in Kyrgyz Republic with
overseas nursing researchers. In addition, we attended research presentations
in various fields and shared our opinions. We could identify the spectrum and
global trends of nursing research and we established an international network
of nursing academic research.<o:p></o:p></span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="mso-pagination:widow-orphan;text-autospace:ideograph-numeric ideograph-other;
