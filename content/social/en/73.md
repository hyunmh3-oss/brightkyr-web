<p class="MsoNormal" align="left" style="line-height: normal; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; word-break: keep-all;"><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size: 11pt; font-family: Nunito; letter-spacing: -0.2pt;">On July 21, 2023, a meeting was held between the BK-LUPIC and Sigma Tau
International (STTI) President Kenneth Dion and Chief Executive Officer
Elizabeth Madigan.<o:p></o:p></span></p><p class="MsoNormal" align="left" style="line-height: normal; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; word-break: keep-all;"><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size: 11pt; font-family: Nunito; letter-spacing: -0.2pt;">At this meeting, the BK-LUPIC shared the goals and promotion status of the
Kyrgyz Republic project and discussed the possibility of cooperation, including
opening a chapter in Central Asia to secure a foundation for growth in nursing
science.<o:p></o:p></span></p><p class="MsoNormal" align="left" style="line-height: normal; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; word-break: keep-all;"><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size: 11pt; font-family: Nunito; letter-spacing: -0.2pt;">What BK-LUPIC is pursuing through its current project is to improve the
overall status of society based on improving the capabilities of nurses in
Kyrgyz Republic, and ultimately improve the level of health care and health
indicators. In response, the STTI delegation said the BK-LUPIC project is a
meaningful project that contributes to the development of nursing education and
practical work. They also introduced various opportunities that can be
supported, saying that there is a high link between the project currently being
carried out by STTI and the goal of this project.<o:p></o:p></span></p><p class="MsoNormal" align="left" style="line-height: normal; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; word-break: keep-all;"><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size: 11pt; font-family: Nunito; letter-spacing: -0.2pt;">Academic networks formed with people of common interest are the foundation
for sharing and discussing academic achievements and finding effective ways to
solve problems given to us. Establishing a global network of nursing science
exchanges in Central Asia, including Kyrgyzstan, where nursing is recognized as
a field of study to solve health and medical problems, will be an important
resource for forming sustainable and influential nursing education and
practice.<o:p></o:p></span></p><p>

various opportunities that will drive the growth of nursing and health care in
Kyrgyzstan and Central Asia and take the lead in creating another opportunity.<o:p></o:p></span></p><p class="MsoNormal" align="left" style="line-height: normal; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; word-break: keep-all;"><img src="/images/uploaded/summernote/202308/b71f19e14f2a5bb377326463cafd1f6a.jpg" style="width: 800px;"><img src="/images/uploaded/summernote/202308/5eed98827439a39cc5a51dccf8f1115c.jpg" style="width: 800px;"><img src="/images/uploaded/summernote/202308/a79bae0ff5bd9cab0884af81b6a875d5.jpg" style="width: 800px;"><img src="/images/uploaded/summernote/202308/630b334ba72741e90baea52746bee363.jpg" style="width: 800px;"><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size: 11pt; font-family: Nunito; letter-spacing: -0.2pt;"><br></span></p>
