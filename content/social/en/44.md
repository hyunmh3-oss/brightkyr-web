Also, a student of the Department of Nursing presented his view about preceptors and practice instructors, how effectively and how it helps during the practice.&nbsp;</p><p>We would like to thank all professors and speakers for the informative lecture.</p><p>Thank you very much for your interest, support, and participation, despite your busy schedule.
