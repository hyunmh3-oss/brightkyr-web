<p>“Былпылдак(Bylpyldak)” felt book class in Ak-Orgo.&nbsp;</p><p>CHW members actively teaching school children’s and mom's how to make “Былпылдак(Bylpyldak)” felt book.</p><p>The creative and hardworking of school children’s, mothers and CHW are very impressive.&nbsp;</p><p>This felt book was first developed in 2018 by nursing school students of Kyung Hee University represented KIDS(Kyrgyzstan Infant and toddler Development Support) project. Under this project, students developed a number of fascinating tools, including “Былпылдак(Bylpyldak)” felt book for parents to help them play with children in early development stages.</p><div><img src="/images/uploaded/summernote/202302/3ee44cc6b73ef74af92433ee999c70e9.jpg" style="width: 25%;"><img src="/images/uploaded/summernote/202302/d968116133404190dcee8591bc1ffab9.jpg" style="width: 25%;"></div><div><br></div>
