<p class="MsoNormal" align="left"><span lang="EN-US">The
Bright Kyrgyzstan-LUPIC team from KHU and KSMA jointly organized the October
2023 Faculty Development Workshop, which took place on October 2, 2023. The
primary objective of this workshop was to share the experiences gained from the
previous Faculty Development Workshop and International Conference on Research
Capacity Enhancement held in July 2023. The workshop ultimately aimed to
establish effective strategies for enhancing the research capacity of faculty
members and students at KSMA.<o:p></o:p></span></p><p class="MsoNormal" align="left"><span lang="EN-US"><br></span></p><p class="MsoNormal" align="left"><span lang="EN-US">A total
of 143 participants attended this workshop, including KSMA faculty members from
various departments, students, including those in the new Bachelor's degree
program in Nursing, and clinical nurses enrolled in transfer programs.<o:p></o:p></span></p><p class="MsoNormal" align="left"><span lang="EN-US"><br></span></p><p class="MsoNormal" align="left"><span lang="EN-US">Faculty
members who are part of the BK-LUPIC team and had previously participated in
the July Faculty Development Workshop took the stage to present their insights
and observations regarding healthcare research trends. Their presentations shed
light on the crucial roles of nurses in this field, emphasizing the necessity
of nursing and healthcare research within the Kyrgyz Republic. The
presentations also highlighted the acquisition and utilization of resources for
research, encouraging all participants to plan for their research needs.&nbsp;</span></p><p class="MsoNormal" align="left"><span lang="EN-US">Moreover, the workshop included discussions about global collaborative research
undertaken by KHU and KSMA, which were shared at the international conference,
extending an invitation to all participants to join these endeavors.<o:p></o:p></span></p><p class="MsoNormal" align="left"><span lang="EN-US"><br></span></p><p class="MsoNormal" align="left"><span lang="EN-US">The
workshop clearly demonstrated a high level of enthusiasm and interest among
both faculty members and students in enhancing their research capabilities and
seizing opportunities for participation in future collaborative research
projects.<o:p></o:p></span></p><p class="MsoNormal" align="left"><span lang="EN-US"><br></span></p><p>







</p><p class="MsoNormal" align="left"><span lang="EN-US">Enhancing
the research capacity of faculty members, students, and healthcare providers,
including nurses, has the potential not only to raise awareness about the
significance of nursing and healthcare research in the Kyrgyz Republic but also
to foster and promote academic research, ultimately broadening the scope of
nursing research.<b><o:p></o:p></b></span></p><p class="MsoNormal" align="left"><span lang="EN-US"><br></span></p><p class="MsoNormal" align="left"><img src="/images/uploaded/summernote/202310/abeede3513510a3b84d4b910e1d368e3.jpg" style="width: 800px;"><span lang="EN-US"><br></span></p><p class="MsoNormal" align="left"><img src="/images/uploaded/summernote/202310/faf83cf756eb8ac2a0410c8c56380e6e.jpg" style="width: 800px;"><br></p><p class="MsoNormal" align="left"><img src="/images/uploaded/summernote/202310/a43c1e9d2f1d87882d2243fe4cfa77e6.jpg" style="width: 800px;"><br></p>
