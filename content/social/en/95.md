<p class="MsoNormal"><img src="/images/uploaded/summernote/202403/f5bcc1c6ca8490299b5b53e8b69afb85.jpg" style="width: 607px;"><span lang="EN-US" style="font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;,serif"><br></span></p><p class="MsoNormal"><span lang="EN-US" style="font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;,serif">Professor
Hyunsook Shin(from the College of Nursing Science at Kyung Hee University) was
awarded "The Merit of KSMA!" (Memorable Medal) by the Rector of
Kyrgyz State Medical Academy (KSMA) Prof. Indira Kudaibergenova on January
26th.<o:p></o:p></span></p><p class="MsoNormal"><span lang="EN-US" style="font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;,serif">Prof.
Indira Kudaibergenova, Rector I.K. Akhunbaev Kyrgyz State Medical Academy
(KSMA), expressed her gratitude during a meeting of the academic council for
nursing education, stating that thanks to the efforts of the BK-LUPIC team and
Professor Hyunsook Shin, the introduction of the nursing bachelor's program was
swiftly advanced. She particularly highlighted the systematic approach of the
BK-LUPIC team, rooted in a deep understanding of Kyrgyzstan, which greatly
enhanced the value and outcomes of the cooperation project in the Kyrgyz
Republic.<o:p></o:p></span></p><p>



</p><p class="MsoNormal"><span lang="EN-US" style="font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;,serif">Professor
Hyunsook Shin has been engaged in international cooperation projects with
Kyrgyzstan since 2014, serving as the Project Manager of the BK-LUPIC team,
dedicated to introducing advanced international standards in nursing education
to the Kyrgyz Republic since its inception&nbsp;in&nbsp;2022.<o:p></o:p></span></p>
