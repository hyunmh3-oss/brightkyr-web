<p class="MsoNormal"><span lang="EN-US">Members of the BK-LUPIC project team and
the GKNF’ met with the Kyrgyz Nursing Association. They shared their
impressions of attending the 4th International Nursing Forum held on the same
day and together celebrated the introduction of Bachelor's degree in nursing in
the Kyrgyz Republic.<o:p></o:p></span></p><p>

</p><p class="MsoNormal"><span lang="EN-US">They agreed to continue their joint efforts
to enhance the social status of nurses in the Kyrgyz Republic, such as
cooperating in establishing a Qualification Framework in the nursing field
which is currently under development. They also discussed the possibility of
connection of infection management and ICT education programs that the Nursing
Association is implementing for nurses. BK-LUPIC will work together for the
improvement of the quality of nursing and healthcare in the Kyrgyz Republic.<o:p></o:p></span></p><p class="MsoNormal"><span lang="EN-US"><br></span></p><p class="MsoNormal"><img src="/images/uploaded/summernote/202308/74997c97c09a8d53bfd5815cb065d4c6.jpg" style="width: 695.139px;"><span lang="EN-US"><br></span></p><p class="MsoNormal"><img src="/images/uploaded/summernote/202308/130e0abbc7738b437b36c1865656179d.jpg" style="width: 714.028px;"><br></p>
