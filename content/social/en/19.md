<p>“The Book is the Source of Knowledge” event was held at the Ak-Ordo Child Health Promotion center by the CHW members.</p><p>The main goals of this event were to introduce the world of books to children from an early age, expand knowledge, encourage them to read, instill love and respect for the books, and spend time together with their parents.</p><p>During the event, mothers read books for the children and discussed the story. They also painted the characters of the stories and played interesting games.&nbsp;</p><p>&nbsp;Preschoolers can come to the center with their parents to read books. They can even borrow books for a few days.</p><div><a href="https://www.facebook.com/brightkyrgyzstan/videos/2331952853531827/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Watch the video on Facebook</a><br></div>
