<p class="MsoNormal" style="line-height: 2;"><font color="#000000" face="Noto Sans KR">The 4th educational session, co-organized by BK-LUPIC and the Electronic Health(eHealth) Center under the Ministry of Health of the Kyrgyz Republic, was held on March 23rd, 2023, at the Kyrgyz State Medical Academy conference hall. The event was attended by key stakeholders, including Nurida Jusupbekova (Head of the Department of Medical education and science, Ministry of Health), Chingiz Beksultanov (Deputy director of the eHealth center), Dzhumalieva Gulmira (Representatives of TFT of KSMA-LUPIC &amp; Vice-Rector, KSMA), TFT members, deputy directors of Nursing and deputy directors of the family medicine center, IT specialists, Lee Seon-hee (Member of Official Development Committee, GKNF), among others, both offline and online.</font></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="line-height: 2;"><font color="#000000" face="Noto Sans KR" style="font-size: 0.9rem; letter-spacing: -0.02em;">The session featured three presentations, including the national health information system in the Kyrgyz Republic and the ICT competencies of Nurses.&nbsp;</font><span style="font-size: 0.9rem; letter-spacing: -0.02em; color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: &quot;Noto Sans KR&quot;;">An "Outpatient card system" was presented during the event, and the need for a nursing information system was confirmed. The other presentation was centered on strategies to utilize health information in nurses' competencies and research through this system. After a thorough discussion, the need for a health information system and improvement plans was derived.</span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="line-height: 1.5;"><br></p><p class="MsoNormal"><br></p><p class="MsoNormal"><img src="/images/uploaded/summernote/202303/9efcaf8dcd988312e246c93c9cd10f5c.jpg" style="width: 940px;"><br></p><p class="MsoNormal"><img src="/images/uploaded/summernote/202303/8a4173f4c741f7c5d695b3bd525373fd.jpg" style="width: 940px;"><br></p>
