<p class="MsoNormal"><span lang="EN-US">&nbsp;After engaging in online team activities with
our esteemed Korean nursing education experts and KSMA faculties since May, we

discussions.<o:p></o:p></span></p><p class="MsoNormal"><span lang="EN-US">&nbsp;</span></p><p class="MsoNormal"><span lang="EN-US">Our ultimate aim is to establish a robust
foundation for a high-quality education program, gearing up for the launch of

September.&nbsp;</span></p><p class="MsoNormal"><span lang="EN-US">&nbsp;</span></p><p>
