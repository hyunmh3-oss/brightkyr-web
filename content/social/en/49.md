<p>Hello! This is the BK GES project team.</p><p>Our 3rd-year nursing education improvement project, "Korea-Kyrgyz joint research for Kyrgyz nursing education policy establishment", which is conducted with the Ministry of Health of the Kyrgyz Republic(hereinafter MOH KR) from April 2021 was completed successfully. The current status of nursing education policy in the Kyrgyz Republic was scrutinized and the demands of stakeholders in the nursing education policy of KR, such as nurses, nursing educators, and authorities of related associations and government, were deeply explored. Based on these results, we derived a strategic suggestion for improving nursing education policies in KR. The Preliminary policy suggestion was shared through the last public hearing and confirmed the resolution through discussion.
