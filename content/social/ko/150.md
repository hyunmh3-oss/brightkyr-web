<p><b><font color="#0000ff"><span style="font-size: 14.4px;">Shin, H., Lee, Y., Lee, S. J., &amp; Jang, Y. K. (2019). Evaluation of a community-based child (infants and toddlers) health promotion pilot project in a migrant village in Kyrgyzstan. <i>Child Health Nursing Research, 25</i>(4), 406-416. </span><a href="https://doi.org/10.4094/chnr.2019.25.4.406" target="_blank" style="">https://doi.org/10.4094/chnr.2019.25.4.406</a></font></b></p><p><span style="font-size: 14.4px;"><font color="#000000"><br></font></span></p><p><span style="font-size: 14.4px;"><b><font color="#000000">Purpose</font></b></span></p><p><font color="#000000"><span style="letter-spacing: -0.02em;">Children in migrant villages in Kyrgyzstan have a high incidence of anemia and need effective health promotion programs. The purpose of this study was to evaluate a pilot study of a health promotion project for infants and toddlers based on community participation.</span><br></font></p><p><font color="#000000"><span style="letter-spacing: -0.02em;"><b>Methods&nbsp;</b></span><br></font></p><p><span style="font-size: 14.4px;"><font color="#000000">This was a retrospective study conducted to evaluate the effectiveness of the project. The project was carried out by nurses at a public health center, community health workers, and local residents in a migrant village. After the operational system of the project was established, health assessments, child-care education, provision of vouchers for iron supplements, and activities to improve residents' awareness were conducted during 6 months among 100 children, of whom 85 were finally analyzed.</font></span></p><p><font color="#000000"><span style="letter-spacing: -0.02em;"><b>Results</b></span><br></font></p><p><font color="#000000"><span style="letter-spacing: -0.02em;">In international health projects, close cooperation of the project team with community residents and health workers is important. Access to the community-based program was feasible in the socially and economically poor migrant village, and improvements were shown in children's anemia and awareness of health care.</span><br></font></p><p><font color="#000000"><span style="letter-spacing: -0.02em;"><b>Conclusion</b></span><br></font></p><p><span style="letter-spacing: -0.02em;"><font color="#000000">By focusing on the effective aspects of this preliminary project, plans to utilize community health workers and promotion strategies can be added to the main project to improve health promotion among children in this area.</font></span><br></p><p><span style="letter-spacing: -0.02em;"><font color="#000000"><br></font></span></p><p><span style="letter-spacing: -0.02em;"><font color="#000000"><br></font></span></p><p><img src="/images/uploaded/summernote/202302/e3c99f91498187c313f7bb89675b5d98.jpg" style="width: 869px;"><span style="letter-spacing: -0.02em;"><font color="#000000"><br></font></span></p>
